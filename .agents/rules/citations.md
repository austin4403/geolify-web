# Data Sourcing & Citations Legal Guardrail

Whenever any external data, geographic map tiles, raster/vector GIS endpoints, or third-party datasets are pulled from a URL into this repository:

1. **Mandatory Documentation**: You MUST ALWAYS record the exact direct URL/endpoint in [CITATIONS.md](file:///home/austin/Projects/Geolify/geolify-web/CITATIONS.md).
2. **Attribution & Licensing**: Include the legal owner/provider, terms of use, and license type (e.g. CC-BY, ODbL, Esri Master Agreement).
3. **On-Canvas Notice**: Ensure any mandatory provider attribution strings are preserved in the MapLibre layers or UI controls.
